# Suggested figure captions

One sentence per figure, to adapt in the report. Each line also states how many random splits the
figure is based on. All figures exist as `.pdf` (for LaTeX) and `.png`.

| Figure file | Splits | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|
| `e1_label_scarcity_cora` | 3 (seeds 0-2) | Test accuracy on Cora as the share of labelled training nodes grows from 1% to 60% (validation fixed at 20%, log-scaled x-axis), mean over 3 random splits with ±1 std bands: GCN leads at every share up to 30%, and MLP + C&S only draws level with it at 60%. |
| `e1_label_scarcity_citeseer` | 3 (seeds 0-2) | The same label-scarcity experiment on Citeseer (mean over 3 splits, ±1 std bands): GCN stays ahead of MLP + C&S at every training share, although the gap shrinks from 5.3 points at 1% to 0.9 points at 60%. |
| `e2_gain_vs_homophily` | 10 (seeds 0-9) | Accuracy gain of MLP + C&S over the plain MLP (mean ± std of the per-split difference over 10 splits) against edge homophily: the gain is large on the three homophilous citation graphs, near zero on Chameleon (h = 0.23) and negative on Texas (h = 0.06). |
| `e2_accuracy_vs_alpha` | 10 (seeds 0-9) | MLP + S test accuracy at a fixed smoothing strength α_smooth (no validation choice; α = 0 is the plain MLP), mean over 10 splits with ±1 std bands: on the homophilous graphs smoothing helps up to α ≈ 0.8-0.9, on Texas accuracy falls steadily as α grows, and at α = 0.99 accuracy drops sharply on every other graph. |
| `e5_gain_vs_homophily_all` | 10 for Cora, Citeseer, Pubmed, Texas, Chameleon; 3 for Photo, Computers | Gain of MLP + C&S over MLP against edge homophily for all seven datasets (LastFM Asia was unavailable), mean ± std over splits: the two new Amazon co-purchase graphs (Photo +4.0, Computers +8.8 points) fall in line with the other homophilous graphs. |
| `e6_gain_by_degree` | 5 (seeds 0-4), test nodes pooled | Accuracy change of MLP + C&S over MLP for test nodes grouped by degree (pooled over 5 splits; faded bars have fewer than 20 nodes, a short tick marks exactly zero change): degree alone does not decide the outcome, since Cora and Citeseer gain at every degree while Texas loses at every degree. |
| `e6_gain_by_local_homophily` | 5 (seeds 0-4), test nodes pooled | The same accuracy change grouped by local homophily, the share of a node's neighbours with its own label (pooled over 5 splits; faded bars < 20 nodes): on every graph, nodes whose neighbours mostly disagree with them are broken by C&S (−6 to −20 points) and nodes whose neighbours mostly agree gain 12-20 points. |
| `e8_label_noise_cora` | 3 (seeds 0-2) | Clean-test accuracy on Cora when a share p of the training labels is flipped to a random wrong class (validation and test labels clean), mean over 3 splits with ±1 std bands: the MLP loses 30 points by p = 40%, MLP + C&S loses 8.5 (its line coincides with MLP + S because Correct changes nothing), and GCN only 2.2. |
| `e8_label_noise_citeseer` | 3 (seeds 0-2) | The same label-noise experiment on Citeseer (mean over 3 splits, ±1 std bands): from p = 0 to 0.4 the MLP loses 23.4 points, MLP + C&S 11.3, LP 9.5 and GCN only 3.0. |
| `q_tsne_stages_cora` | 1 (seed 0) | t-SNE embeddings of Cora's 543 test nodes (same settings, perplexity 30, fixed random seed) from (a) the MLP output Z, (b) after Correct and (c) after Smooth, coloured by true class: Correct barely moves the MLP's outputs, while Smooth pulls each class into its own compact cluster (72.4% → 85.8% test accuracy). |
| `q_tsne_stages_texas` | 1 (seed 0) | The same three t-SNE panels for Texas's 38 test nodes (perplexity 10, fixed random seed), the failure case: the class clusters visible in the MLP output are scrambled after Smooth, and test accuracy falls from 63.2% to 57.9%. |
| `q_outcomes_cora` | 1 (seed 0) | Cora test nodes at their t-SNE position from the MLP output, marked by what C&S did to each one: 87 fixed (wrong → right) against 14 broken (right → wrong), with 379 staying right and 63 staying wrong. |
| `q_outcomes_texas` | 1 (seed 0) | The same outcome map for Texas: C&S fixes only 1 test node and breaks 3, with 21 staying right and 13 staying wrong. |
